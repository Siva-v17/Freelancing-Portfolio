FAQ

Questions I get asked before kickoff.

How long does a typical project take?

+

A marketing site usually takes 2–3 weeks. Adding a dashboard or auth flow typically adds 1–2 weeks depending on scope.

Do you work with existing designs?

+

Yes — I can build from a Figma file, or design the site myself as part of the engagement if you don't have one yet.

Can you fix mobile issues on a site I already have?

+

Definitely — responsive QA and media-query fixes are one of my most common requests. Send the URL and I'll audit it.

Do you handle backend and hosting too?

+

My focus is frontend, but I regularly wire up to APIs, Firebase, or a backend your team already has, and can advise on hosting.

What do you need from me to start?

+

A short brief, any brand assets you already have (logo, colors), and real copy where possible — placeholder text slows things down.
